Best movies and series of Abdi

My best movies

Extraction

Extraction is a hit 2020 action-thriller directed by Sam Hargrave and produced by the Russo brothers. It stars Chris Hemsworth as Tyler Rake, a fearless black-market mercenary tasked with rescuing the kidnapped son of an imprisoned international crime lord in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Furious 7

In Furious 7 (2015), the crew reunites to face Deckard Shaw (Jason Statham), a rogue assassin seeking revenge for his comatose brother. To stop him and rescue a kidnapped hacker with a world-tracking program, Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel) and Brian O'Conner (Paul Walker) must work with a covert ops leader.

The gray man

The Gray Man is a 2022 Netflix action-thriller directed by the Russo brothers. It follows Court Gentry (Ryan Gosling), a legendary CIA black-ops mercenary who accidentally uncovers the agency's dark secrets. Framed and hunted, he goes on the run from a psychopathic former colleague and a global network of assassins.

My best series

Game of thrones

Game of Thrones is an epic fantasy drama that follows the violent political struggles among noble families in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos as they vie for control of the Iron Throne. Alongside the human wars for power, the realm faces an ancient, supernatural threat from the icy north.

Money heist

Money Heist (La Casa de Papel) is a Spanish crime-drama following a mysterious mastermind known as "The Professor". He recruits eight skilled thieves to pull off the biggest heists in recorded history—printing billions at the Royal Mint of Spain and later breaking into the Bank of Spain. The crew hides behind signature red jumpsuits and Salvador Dalí masks.

The last ship

The Last Ship is an action-packed, post-apocalyptic drama centering on the crew of the fictional guided-missile destroyer USS Nathan James. After a global viral pandemic wipes out over 80% of the world's population, the isolated crew races against time to develop a cure, evade hostile forces, and save humanity from extinction.
